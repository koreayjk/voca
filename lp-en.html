Questions

Before you start

Which languages does it support?

The app interface is available in English, Korean, Chinese, Japanese and Spanish. The official wordbooks teach English, with meanings written in your own language. You can also build your own wordbook in any language pair by photographing a page.

Do I need a paper book?

No. The official wordbooks — 4,800 words across twelve CEFR levels — are free and already in the app. The camera is there for when you want to study a book you already own.

How is this different from Anki?

You do not build the deck. Photograph a page and the cards exist, complete with meanings, example sentences and pronunciation. The scheduling is fixed and automatic — nothing to configure.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Overdue reviews wait for you instead of resetting. There is no streak to break and no penalty.

Is my data safe if I switch phones?

Sign in with the same account and your words, progress and review schedule follow you — across devices, and between the web and the app.
