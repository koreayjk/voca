Account & Data Deletion Request

App: IM VOCA · Developer: IM AMERICA GROUP CORP
How to delete your IM VOCA account and all associated data.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate, recommended)

  1. Open the IM VOCA app and sign in.
  2. Tap My ▾ at the top right of the bookshelf screen.
  3. Select 🗑️ Delete Account at the bottom of the menu.
  4. Type DELETE as prompted, then tap Delete permanently.
✅ Your account and data are deleted immediately, and any active subscription is cancelled automatically. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot sign in, email us from the address used to register, with the subject "Account deletion request".
  • Email: imamerica2414@gmail.com
  • Processing time: within 7 business days of confirmation
