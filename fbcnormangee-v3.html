Living Out the Great Commission

Our Faith

What We Believe

Core Beliefs

Our beliefs are grounded in the Bible — the inspired, inerrant Word of God. These core doctrines shape our faith, worship, and life together as a church.

✝The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's written, inspired Word to man — equal in all parts and without error in its original manuscript.

✝One God

We believe there is one God who exists as three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — the holy Trinity, co-equal and co-eternal.

✝Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God — born of a virgin, lived sinlessly, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and will return to Earth.

✝The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit indwells and empowers believers, guiding them to live the Christian life and bear witness to Christ.

Salvation & The Church

✝Salvation

Sin caused separation from God. Only through God's grace — believing in Jesus's sinless life, death on the cross, and resurrection — is that relationship restored.

✝The Church

The church is the body and bride of Christ — God's vessel for revealing His purpose on Earth and a gathering place for believers to grow together in faith.
