“Mom, what does this mean?”
…for the third time this week.

They look it up. The dictionary gives them the wrong meaning. They give up on the book.

Here is a real page, and what actually happens.

The page

The Wind in the Willows, chapter 1 — standard school reading

What one photo gives you

Only the words that actually stop them.

Not a word list. The words on their page, with the meaning that fits that sentence.

Hard
Hang figurative
To suspend or be suspended from above.
Used as an exclamation to express dismissal or annoyance. Here, “Hang spring-cleaning!” means “Forget about spring-cleaning!”
Literally: To suspend from above. → used differently here
Hard
bolted
Past tense of ‘bolt’: to run away suddenly and quickly.
The Mole suddenly and quickly ran out of his house.
Easy
put on phrasal verb
To place clothing or accessories on one’s body.
The Mole left without dressing himself in his coat.
Literally: To place something on top of another object. → used differently here
