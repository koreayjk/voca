The technology

One film. Three things your line no longer needs.

Conventional SPC/LVT production applies a clear top layer, runs a UV coating pass, and blends in stone powder to cut gloss. Our pre-coated film removes all three steps.

No UV coating line

The protective coating is laminated into the film itself. You bond it to the board — no separate UV curing pass, no coating equipment on your floor.

~₩3B capex avoided per line

No stone-powder matting

Most matte floors lower gloss with stone powder, which scratches white over time. Our film reaches a true super-matte finish without it — so scuffs don't show as bright marks.

No white scratch bloom

Supply you control

Because we hold the coated film, output and supply volume stay adjustable. Order by the container, scale as demand grows — a simple, repeatable B2B supply relationship.
